Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how PixelCraft TV Care (India Electronics Hub) collects, utilizes, and safeguards customer data.

📅 Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PixelCraft TV Care ("we," "our," or "us"), a trade brand operated by registered proprietorship India Electronics Hub (Sole Proprietor: Pankaj Kumar, GSTIN: 09HMVPK3965C1ZE). We operate the website and provide television repair services across Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, disclose, and protect your personal information when you contact us, visit our website, or schedule a repair service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Customer Provided Information

When you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp, or through our service booking forms, we may collect:

  • Full Name: For appointment booking and service job sheet records.
  • Phone Number: For technician dispatch coordination and service updates.
  • Email Address: For delivery of digital tax invoices, repair estimates, and warranty certificates.
  • Premises Address: For doorstep service dispatch by our local technician.
  • Television Technical Details: Brand, screen size, model number, and symptoms of the reported fault.

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

When you browse our website, limited technical data may be automatically logged (browser type, IP address, device operating system, pages viewed) strictly for operational website security and performance monitoring.

3. How We Use Collected Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate business and fulfillment purposes:

  • To schedule, coordinate, and perform television repair appointments at your doorstep or workshop.
  • To generate GST-compliant service invoices and issue valid warranty certificates.
  • To provide customer support and process warranty claims if issues reoccur.
  • To comply with statutory accounting and tax regulations in India.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks.

4. Information Disclosure & Sharing

We share customer data only under necessary, restricted circumstances:

  • Field Technicians: Your name, phone number, and service address are shared with the designated technician assigned to your service call.
  • Statutory Authorities: When required by Indian law, GST compliance rules, or official court summons.

5. Data Security

We employ administrative and technical precautions to safeguard your contact details and transaction records. Only authorized technical and administrative personnel have access to service logs.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Grievances

If you have any questions regarding your personal information, or wish to update or delete your service record, please contact our designated privacy coordinator:
