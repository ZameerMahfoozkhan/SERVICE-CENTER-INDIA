1. Purpose & Scope

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to all television repair and maintenance services provided by India Electronic Hub Service Center (Proprietor: Pankaj Kumar, GSTIN: 09HMVPK3965C1ZE), trading as PixelCraft TV Care across Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Our priority is 100% transparent and satisfactory service delivery.

2. Appointment Booking & Cancellation

  • Cancellation Prior to Dispatch: If you cancel your service booking before our technician is dispatched to your premises, no fee whatsoever is charged.
  • On-Site Technical Diagnosis: Once our technician arrives, a full diagnostic assessment is performed to inspect and identify the exact electronic fault.
  • Customer Approval: Our technician details the required component repair before starting work. You have complete freedom to approve or decline the service.
  • Declining Service: If you choose not to proceed with the repair after receiving the technician's diagnosis, no repair work will be undertaken.

3. Repair Resolution & Refund Eligibility

We are committed to delivering reliable workmanship and satisfactory repair outcomes for every customer:

  • Re-Inspection Support: If an electronic fault persists after service completion, our technician will re-inspect the television to diagnose and resolve the issue.
  • Service Fee Refund: In the rare event that our technical team is unable to resolve the diagnosed defect after service, a refund of the service labour charge and defective replacement part cost will be evaluated and processed.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds cannot be granted under the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Physical screen shattering, cracks, or liquid spillage caused by external impact or accidental handling after technician completion.
  • Damage caused by ungrounded severe electrical lightening strikes or external household power surges that destroy unrelated internal components.
  • Interference, seal breakage, or tampering by third-party technicians after our service visit.

5. Refund Mode & Timeline

  • Approved refunds are processed within 2 to 5 business days via original payment method (UPI, bank transfer, or cash voucher).
  • A digital refund credit note is issued with reference to your original GST invoice number.
