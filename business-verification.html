3. Business Operations & Service Delivery Model

In response to Google AdWords Business Operations Verification questions regarding business relationships and service fulfillment:

  • Direct Service Provider: We are the direct service provider. We do NOT act as an agency, lead broker, aggregator, or affiliate. All service calls are handled by our own technical coordinators and fulfilled by our in-house technicians.
  • Technician Employment & Training: Our technicians are directly engaged by India Electronics Hub and possess formal training in electronics circuit repair and TV display diagnostics.
  • Doorstep & In-Shop Logistics: Routine component replacements (LED backlights, modular power supply units, sound ICs) are conducted at the customer's doorstep. Delicate motherboard chip replacement or BGA rebonding is handled at our Arya Nagar facility.
  • Billing & Payment Terms: Customers receive an itemized service receipt/invoice with GST details. Payments are accepted via UPI, cash, or bank transfer only after service completion and testing.
  • Quality Standards: High-grade tested replacement components and skilled workmanship are verified on-site before completion.
