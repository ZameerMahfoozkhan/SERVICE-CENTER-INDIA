    • 2. Our Operational Business Model

    For Google Ads Business Operations Verification and for consumer confidence, we provide full disclosure of how our service operates end-to-end:

    A. Direct Doorstep Diagnosis

    When a customer contacts us via call, WhatsApp, or website form, our central coordination desk in Lucknow captures the TV brand, model, and screen symptoms. An in-house certified technician is dispatched directly to the customer's residence with diagnostic tools (multimeter, LED backlight tester, and spare modular boards).

    B. Upfront Transparent Pricing & No Surprise Charges

    A nominal visiting/inspection fee of ₹199 applies to cover technician transit and thorough on-site diagnosis. If the customer approves the repair quote, the entire ₹199 inspection fee is credited/waived against the repair bill. If the customer chooses not to proceed after diagnosis, only the nominal ₹199 inspection fee is payable.

    C. On-Site vs. Workshop Component Repairs

    Over 80% of TV faults (LED backlight strips, power supply capacitor fixes, software flash/updates, speaker replacements) are performed directly at the customer's residence in front of them within 60 to 90 minutes. For microscopic motherboard BGA chip rework, liquid panel bonding, or complex circuit micro-soldering, the unit is safely received at our physical workshop in Arya Nagar, Lucknow with a signed receipt, repaired, and delivered back.

    D. Official Tax Invoice & Warranty Card

    Upon completion and customer testing, we issue a clear service invoice specifying the replacement components used, technician details, and an official 30 to 90-day written warranty on parts and workmanship.

    3. Our Physical Workshop & Facility

    We maintain a dedicated physical workshop and operational testing center in Lucknow, equipped with high-precision electronics diagnostic and repair machinery:

    • SMD & BGA Rework Station: For micro-level motherboard processor and IC repairs without expensive board replacements.
    • Digital Oscilloscopes & Logic Analyzers: For accurate power frequency and signal tracing on smart TV motherboards.
    • Dedicated Clean Bench: For dust-free open-cell LED/LCD panel disassembly, diffuser replacement, and backlight installation.
    • Universal Component Inventory: Over 1,500+ tested replacement parts in stock, including constant-current LED driver boards, T-Con boards, and high-lumen backlight arrays.
