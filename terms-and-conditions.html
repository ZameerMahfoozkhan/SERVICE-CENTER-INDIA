3. Services Provided

We provide diagnostic, component repair, and electronic maintenance services for television receivers and monitors, including:

  • LED backlight strip replacement and optical diffuser alignment.
  • Power Supply Unit (PSU) circuit board capacitor, diode, and regulator restoration.
  • Motherboard and smart processor troubleshooting, firmware flashing, and BGA soldering.
  • Display T-Con board and flat flexible cable (FFC) diagnosis.
  • Speaker and audio output module replacements.

4. Pricing, Inspection Fee & Approval

  • Doorstep Diagnostic Fee: A nominal visiting fee of ₹199 applies when our technician arrives and conducts electrical testing at your premises.
  • 100% Fee Credit: If you approve the quoted repair cost, the entire ₹199 visit fee is credited and deducted from your final repair invoice.
  • Upfront Written Quote: Our technician provides an itemized cost estimate before opening screws or installing components. You are completely free to decline the service at this stage without additional obligation beyond the nominal visit charge.
  • No Hidden Costs: We never apply retroactive or surprise charges. What is agreed upon in the diagnostic estimate is the final payable amount.

5. Written Repair Warranty

  • All replacement components and repair workmanship are covered under our written warranty ranging between 30 to 90 days, as specified on your service invoice.
  • If the identical electronic fault reoccurs within the warranty duration, PixelCraft TV Care will rectify the fault free of charge.
  • Warranty Exclusions: Warranty does not cover accidental physical liquid spillage, external physical screen impact/cracking, erratic high-voltage lightning strikes, or tampering by third-party unauthorized technicians after our visit.
